Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Applies to Openmi CLI and www.openmi.space

Privacy Policy Terms of Service No-Refund Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Openmi (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Openmi CLI application, website, and Developer Console at www.openmi.space.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Data: Your email address, authentication identifiers, and CLI device-login sessions managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Usage & Quota Telemetry: Daily credit balances, daily input/output token counts, lifetime AI token totals, active hourly pass windows, credit and Wallet Coin ledger entries, and referral/invite code activity.
  • Payment Verification Records: Paid passes are processed externally through Sociabuzz. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or banking credentials. When you submit payment proof on /pay, we store your Sociabuzz transaction reference, optional supporter note, and uploaded receipt file (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, or PDF up to 5 MB) solely to verify and activate your pass.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Configuration: If you configure your own third-party AI provider API keys via /provider or your local CLI configuration, those keys are used only to authenticate requests to your chosen provider.
  • Browser Storage: We use essential authentication session storage and local preferences (such as your selected country/currency display preference in localStorage). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

2. Local Files, Prompts & AI Processing

  • Local-First CLI: Openmi CLI runs locally on your machine (Windows, Linux, macOS, or Android Termux). Exact-byte undo checkpoints and workspace files remain on your device.
  • Prompt & Context Transmission: Only the prompts, file snippets, or tool outputs you explicitly send during a session are transmitted to AI model providers to generate responses.
  • Managed vs. BYOK Routing: Requests using Openmi managed credits/passes are routed through Openmi’s backend to upstream AI model providers. Requests using BYOK are sent to your configured third-party provider under that provider’s own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your account and pair CLI devices via openmi login.
  • Enforce daily credit limits, daily token caps, hourly pass windows, and referral anti-abuse rules.
  • Verify Sociabuzz payment receipts and activate 15-day or 30-day Paid Passes and Wallet Coins.
  • Maintain service reliability, prevent fraud or abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Subprocessors

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to operate Openmi:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database storage, and backend edge functions.
  • Vercel: Hosting for the Openmi web console and static assets.
  • Sociabuzz: External payment processing for Paid Passes.
  • Upstream AI Model Providers: Processing prompts for managed models available in the Openmi catalog.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account, usage ledger, and payment verification records for as long as your account is active or as needed to prevent abuse, resolve billing disputes, and comply with legal obligations. You may manage your account or request deletion through Account Settings or by contacting us via our official repository.

6. Security

All traffic between Openmi CLI, www.openmi.space, and our backend is encrypted in transit using TLS (rustls in the CLI). While we apply reasonable technical safeguards, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Openmi after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please open an issue or contact the maintainers via github.com/LOWENDLAGANI/Openmi-cli.
