No-Refund Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Applies to all Openmi Paid Passes and Wallet Coins

Privacy Policy Terms of Service No-Refund Policy
All sales are final. Because Openmi Paid Passes (15-day and 30-day passes) and bonus Wallet Coins provide digital access to managed AI model compute and immediately allocated account quotas, all completed payments made via Sociabuzz are non-refundable.

1. Scope of This Policy

This No-Refund Policy applies to all purchases made for Openmi services on www.openmi.space/pay, including:

  • 15-Day Paid Pass (MYR 14.99, including +2 bonus Wallet Coins).
  • 30-Day Paid Pass (MYR 29.00, including +3 bonus Wallet Coins).
  • Any future digital passes, top-ups, or Wallet Coin bundles offered through Openmi.

2. No Partial Refunds or Proration

  • We do not issue full or partial refunds for unused pass days, unused daily credits, unused daily token allowances, or unused Wallet Coins.
  • You may test Openmi CLI before purchasing using our Free tier (which includes free daily credits resetting at 00:00 UTC) or with your own API keys via BYOK.
  • Account suspension or termination due to a violation of our Terms of Service does not entitle you to a refund.

3. Payment Processing & Currency

Payments are processed externally via Sociabuzz and charged in MYR. Local currency prices displayed on /pay are indicative estimates only. Exchange rate differences, bank fees, or payment processor fees charged by Sociabuzz or your payment provider are outside our control and are non-refundable.

4. Billing Errors & Exceptions

The only exceptions to this No-Refund Policy are:

  • Verified Duplicate Payment: If you were demonstrably charged twice for the exact same pass order due to a technical error and the duplicate pass was not activated or consumed.
  • Unfulfilled Pass Activation: If your verified Sociabuzz payment cannot be fulfilled due to a technical fault on our side, we will either activate the pass manually or resolve the issue with you.
  • Mandatory Law: Where a refund is strictly required by applicable consumer protection law in your jurisdiction.

5. Need Help With a Payment?

If your Sociabuzz payment has not been approved yet or you made a mistake submitting your transaction reference, do not initiate a chargeback before contacting us. Reach out via github.com/LOWENDLAGANI/Openmi-cli with your Openmi account email and Sociabuzz transaction reference so we can assist you promptly.
